01 · AI-enabled operations

AI-Assisted Client Operations

Redesigned a manual, email-driven client connection workflow into an AI-assisted operating system for a regional CRM team.

AI / RAGAgentic workflowDiscoveryHuman-in-the-loop
90%time saved on fully automated cases
70–75%time saved on human-in-the-loop cases
Up to 90%quicker responses for clients / case reporters
MVPworking across initial workflow categories

Discovery

I identified the workflow while scanning teams for AI-optimization opportunities, then interviewed each team member to map the process and its friction points.

Design decision

Automation is matched to certainty and access: the agent can investigate and draft independently, while consequential writes and judgment remain human-owned.

Built

Python, Microsoft Graph, RAG workflow memory, document generation and Salesforce access through MCP. Current phase requires human confirmation before sending.

~100%automation coverage for self-contained cases
~60%coverage where other stakeholders are involved
~30%coverage where Salesforce write access is required
02 · Workflow automation

Client Access Provisioning Automation

Turned a manual enterprise provisioning workflow into a standardized, automated process with measurable cycle-time and accuracy gains.

Process redesignRPAStakeholder discoveryAdoption
2–3h → <5mprocessing time per request
90%reduction in operational dependency
95%form submission accuracy
Same dayprocessing for 10–15 weekly requests

Problem

Requests arrived through unstructured email and required repeated data retrieval, manual form completion and external coordination.

My role

Identified the opportunity, ran discovery across stakeholder groups, defined the solution, partnered with the automation team and managed post-launch adoption issues.

Learning

Automation changes the surrounding behavior too. Standardized inputs and clear escalation paths were necessary to prevent the old manual process from returning around the new automation.

03 · Zero-to-one product

BriefBack — Meeting Intelligence

A personal AI product concept that gives PMs proactive context before recurring stakeholder meetings instead of another transcript afterward.

Product discoveryPRDPrioritizationAI product strategy

Problem

PMs spend significant time reconstructing decisions, open items and commitments before recurring stakeholder meetings.

PM work
